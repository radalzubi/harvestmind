# Model misspecification and recovery

The optimizer no longer tests robustness solely by resampling its own fitted
model. The portfolio contains a stress-envelope network plan, and all potential
promotions pass separate central-model confirmation and operator-declared adverse
models. Adversaries can lower fill, widen leads, raise demand, delay recovery and
force correlated failures; resource scope identifies which alternate routes remain
unaffected. These are assumptions, not calibrated probabilities or Wasserstein DRO.

A candidate must meet service in adverse evaluation, avoid physical violations,
and stay within approved relative cost/service downside tolerance. If the cheapest
candidate fails, other selection-feasible candidates are checked. Unit-fill controls
can never open the gate. A retained incumbent may itself fail stress tests; the
report exposes that, rather than declaring it safe.

When targets are unattainable, separately labelled recovery guidance maximizes
priority-weighted service then minimizes cost. Eligible cancellation, rescheduling,
expediting and de-expediting options compete with transfers and fresh purchases.
Each option requires a current contract; fresh orders without amendment rights do
not gain invented cancellation options on later reviews.

News alone does not change physical yields or trigger purchasing. Licensed physical
and official evidence maps to a disclosed stress adjustment. The available data do
not establish a calibrated news-driven prior or earlier profitable action. Existing
NOAA/PortWatch/AIS/freight rights boundaries remain as documented in v1's source audit.
No paid feed access or historical vintage was invented.
