# DIAL Ventures — Outreach Email

_Single tailored draft. Under 250 words. The goal is one 25-minute call._

DIAL Ventures is a Purdue-affiliated agtech accelerator focused on
food safety, supply-chain shortages, sustainability, environmental
impact, and de-risking agrifood startups through industry partnerships.
HarvestMind aligns directly with the food safety + supply-chain
shortage focus areas.

This is the only outreach email that uses the Version B (venture-track)
framing. Operator emails, professor emails, and connector emails all
stay on Version A.

## Why this email is structured this way

1. **Opens with what we built**, not with a thesis. DIAL sees too
   many "we will be the next $1B agtech company" pitches. Leading
   with a working MVP is differentiation.

2. **Names the wedge price** ($3K–$15K diagnostic). This signals you
   have a near-term revenue path, not just a thesis.

3. **Names the larger thesis** (operational intelligence for
   perishables) but immediately caveats: "we are testing whether."
   Not "we will become."

4. **Uses the correct FSMA-204 timing** (July 20, 2028). Investors
   and accelerators verify these dates; getting it wrong destroys
   credibility instantly.

5. **States explicitly we are not asking for capital today.**
   Removes the most common reason an accelerator email gets
   declined or ignored — perceived premature ask.

6. **Three asks, descending in size**: 25 minutes, one operator
   intro, one advisor/co-founder lead. Easy to say yes to any one.

7. **Closes with the COI/IP language** that we use across all
   Purdue advisor outreach. Signals you are aware of the process
   and not asking them to skip it.

## What to do after a positive reply

If they say yes to a meeting:

- Send the **Version B one-pager** (`venture_track_one_page_memo.md`)
  rendered as PDF, attached, the day before the meeting
- **Do not** send the deck in advance. Save it for the meeting.
- Read `12_month_venture_path.md` the morning of, so you can speak
  to milestones if asked
- Have a printout of the diagnostic HTML report from the sample
  dataset, ready to hand them if they want to see output

## What to do if you get a soft "let me think about it"

- Wait 7 business days
- Send one follow-up: "Following up on the note below — happy to
  send the one-pager if it would help you decide. No pressure."
- After 14 days without reply, move on. Re-approach in 90 days
  with new evidence (first paid pilot, first customer reference,
  etc.) — not before.

## What to do if they say no

- Thank them by reply within 24 hours
- Ask: "If we did one specific thing in the next 90 days, what
  would make you reconsider?"
- Most investors respect this question because it costs them
  nothing to answer and signals you take feedback seriously
- File their answer; revisit with evidence in 4–6 months

## What never to include in the DIAL email

- A funding ask
- A revenue projection
- The word "AI"
- "We are pre-revenue but" (just say "no paid customers yet")
- Any mention of Saudi Arabia, carbon, marketplace, or white-label
- Any claim about specific operator savings ("we identify $X")
- A claim that any specific path (A/B/C) is the right one — let
  the conversation surface their view
