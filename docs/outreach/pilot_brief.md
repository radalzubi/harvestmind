# A Decision Layer for Perishable Supply Chains
*Turning your own operating history into sharper supply and commitment decisions.*

_Universal brief — one standardized attachment for all operator types (distributors, growers, packers, processors, cooperatives, food hubs, foodservice). Personalize the email, keep this attachment the same._

**THE DECISION GAP**
Perishable operators commit to buyers before final supply is certain. Depending on the operation, that supply may be purchased, produced, harvested, processed, or aggregated from multiple suppliers. ERPs and inventory systems record what happened; they rarely recommend what to commit next. A wrong call becomes excess inventory, waste, a shortage, a substitution, or unfilled demand, and the cost is usually scattered across operating reports rather than appearing as one number anyone can manage.

**WHAT THE DIAGNOSTIC MEASURES**
Using records you already keep, we estimate where operating cost or margin may have been lost across:
- Waste, product value, shortages, and unfilled demand
- Supplier fill variability and rejections
- Harvest, packout, production, or processing-yield variability, where relevant
- Buyer fulfillment and substitution
- Available-to-promise performance by product

We quantify each measure only where the available fields support a defensible calculation.

**WHAT THE OPERATOR RECEIVES**
- A quantified baseline of those measures, where your data supports them.
- A historical backtest evaluating how alternative supply, allocation, and commitment rules would have performed against your own operating history.
- A concise results review: findings, assumptions, limitations, and practical implications. A historical backtest is not a guarantee of real-world recovery.

**HOW THE PILOT WORKS**
1. A 15-minute introductory conversation to see whether the problem is relevant to you.
2. A brief review of your available fields and data suitability.
3. If the data is sufficient, a confidential historical diagnostic and backtest.
4. A results discussion covering findings, assumptions, and limitations.

The pilot is free and carries no obligation. This is an early-stage operator pilot to validate the diagnostic using real operating records, not a software sales proposal.

**DATA NEEDED**
The diagnostic generally uses some combination of:
- Buyer orders, requested quantities, commitments, and fulfillment
- Supplier purchase orders and actual receipts
- Producer availability, harvest, production, packout, or processing-yield records, where relevant
- Product identifiers, dates, waste, rejections, and inventory adjustments
- Unit cost and selling price, where available

Not every operator needs every field. We first review data suitability and explain which conclusions the available records can and cannot support. CSV exports are usually sufficient once the fields are confirmed.

**CONFIDENTIALITY**
We can sign a non-disclosure agreement before receiving records. Data is used only for the agreed diagnostic, access is limited to the project team, and transfer and retention procedures are agreed before sharing. Records are deleted or returned at your request.
