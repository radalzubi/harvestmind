## 2. Cold call script (operator version)

**Goal of the call:** one yes to a free historical backtest (6-12 months of
order + delivery CSVs). Not a sale. If they will not share data, get a
30-minute discovery meeting instead.

**Opener (10 seconds):**
"Hi, this is Rad Al Zubi. I'm an industrial engineer out of Purdue. I work
with perishable distributors on waste and shortage costs. Did I catch you
in the middle of receiving, or do you have two minutes?"

(If busy: "When is a better time today or tomorrow?" and get off the phone.)

**The pitch (20 seconds, then stop talking):**
"We built an engine that takes the CSV exports you already have, orders,
deliveries, inventory, and puts dollar figures on what waste, shortages,
and supplier shortfalls actually cost you last year. Then it shows what a
smarter weekly commitment policy would have saved. We run it on your own
history, so the proof comes from your numbers, not our claims."

**The qualifying questions (listen for pain, take notes):**
1. "When you commit to a buyer before your growers confirm, how do you
   decide how much to promise?"
2. "What happened the last time a supplier shorted you on a committed load?"
3. "Where does that cost show up? Does anyone see a number for it?"
4. "What system holds your order and delivery history right now?"

**The ask:**
"Here is what I'd propose. You export six to twelve months of orders and
deliveries as CSVs, we sign an NDA first if you want one, and we run the
backtest at no cost and no obligation. You get a report in dollars on your
own operation. If the number is boring, you never hear from us again. Worth
a look?"

**Objection handling:**
- "We already have a system." → "Most operators we talk to have a system of
  record. This is not a system of record. It answers one question those
  systems do not: how much should you commit next week, given what your
  suppliers actually deliver. The backtest will show whether your current
  process is already optimal. If it is, that is worth knowing too."
- "Our data is a mess." → "Messy is normal and honestly expected. Cleaning
  and structuring it is the first thing the engine does. A plain CSV export
  is enough."
- "How much does it cost?" → "The backtest costs nothing. If the findings
  are worth acting on, we talk about what an ongoing tool would look like.
  There is no commitment past the report."
- "Send me something." → "Happy to. I'll send a one-page brief today. Can
  we put fifteen minutes on the calendar for Thursday so I can walk you
  through it?" (Always convert to a scheduled slot.)
- "We're too small / too big." → Small: "Small is actually ideal, the
  report is sharper when one person knows the whole operation." Big: "Then
  the absolute dollars are bigger. One product line is enough to start."

**Never say:** AI platform, stochastic optimization, "we'll save you $X"
(no customer-validated savings yet), "we've validated with N operators"
(we have not), or anything about fundraising.

**Close every call with a concrete next step and a date.**
