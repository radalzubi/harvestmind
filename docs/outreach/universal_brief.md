# A Decision Layer for Perishable Supply Chains
### What to buy, what to commit, what to promise, before supply is certain

---

**THE DECISION GAP**

Perishable operators commit supply to buyers before they know what their own
suppliers will deliver. ERPs and inventory systems record what happened. They
were not built to recommend what to commit next. That pre-commitment window has
no standard decision layer, and McKinsey has found that most supply-chain
planning still runs on spreadsheets. In perishables a wrong commitment resolves
one of two ways, wasted product or a missed order, and the cost disappears into
margin rather than onto a line anyone can manage.

---

**WHY PERISHABLES, SPECIFICALLY**

The product carries a clock, margins are thin, and the decision is irreversible
once made. BCG's recent agrifood work identifies coordination failure, not only
climate, as a primary driver of fresh-food supply-chain fragility. The FSMA-204
traceability rule is now forcing operators to structure the same supplier and
lot-level data this decision layer runs on. The data is becoming available. The
decision support has not caught up.

---

**WHAT WE DO**

The demand and inventory intelligence that has reached grocery retail's fresh
departments has not reached the suppliers and distributors who feed them. That
is where we sit. We turn the messy CSVs an operator already exports into
structured, organized operational data, then run three things on top of it. A
diagnostic that shows, in dollars, where margin is lost: waste, shortage,
supplier fill and rejection, buyer fulfillment, and available-to-promise by
product. A compliance layer that structures and checks the FSMA-204 lot-level
traceability operators now have to produce anyway. And a decision layer that
recommends how much to commit to each supplier and buyer and quantifies the
overbuy and underbuy risk on each.

We prove the decision layer by backtesting. We replay an operator's own history
and measure what the recommendations would have saved, so the trust comes from
their numbers rather than our claims. We have no live customers yet.
