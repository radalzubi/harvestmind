# Purdue Advisor Outreach Emails

Five tailored drafts. Each under 220 words. Each frames the ask around two
things we genuinely need help with at this stage:

1. **Model pressure-test** — is the diagnostic measuring the right things?
2. **Business-model question** — should this become a SaaS, a productized
   diagnostic, or an ERP add-on, and what evidence would tell us?

We are NOT yet pitching a market-size story or claiming customer traction.
Anyone who reads the emails should walk away with the same understanding
that's in `advisor_one_page_memo.md`: MVP is built, three paths are
possible, real operator data is the next bottleneck.
