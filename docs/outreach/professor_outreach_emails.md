# Purdue Advisor Outreach Emails

Five tailored drafts. Each under 220 words. Each frames the ask as a 25-minute
feedback conversation, not a commitment, and explicitly avoids hype.

Before sending: read each draft aloud, verify the professor's current title
and most recent relevant work, and update the subject line if a Purdue or
DIAL contact is making the warm introduction.

## Sequencing and follow-up

1. Send in this order: Khir, Monaco/DIAL, Fiechter, Deering, Torres
2. Stagger by 2–3 days so you can incorporate feedback between meetings
3. Follow up once after 7 business days if no reply — one short polite line
4. After each meeting, send a thank-you note within 24 hours summarizing
   the three most useful pieces of feedback (not the whole conversation)
5. Track meetings in a single Notion/Sheet — name, date, top feedback,
   open intro requests, follow-up commitment
