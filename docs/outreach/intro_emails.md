Hello,

My name is Rad Alzubi. Mustafa Sabieha and I are industrial engineers from Purdue, and we are building a decision tool for perishable distributors.

Perishable operators commit to buyers before they know exactly what their suppliers will deliver, so a wrong call becomes either waste or a missed order, and the cost usually hides in margin. We help measure that cost and tighten the decision, and we do it from records you already have.

Before asking anything of you, we would like to be useful. If you are open to it, we will put together a short analysis of your operation, free and with no obligation. Your data stays yours. We keep it confidential, use it only for this, and are glad to sign an NDA first.
