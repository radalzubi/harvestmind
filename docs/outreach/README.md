# Outreach — PROJECT A (AGRICULTURE, US) ONLY

Everything in this directory belongs to the **agriculture** project: US
Midwest perishable operators, produce distributors, aggregators and food
hubs, plus the Purdue academic and extension network.

**This is not Project B material.** The commercial and regional
distribution project, starting in Jordan, has different prospects,
different uncertainty (route and transit rather than weather) and different
claims. Do not send these briefs to those contacts, and do not quote the
Indiana synthetic results in commercial materials.

See `docs/projects/README.md` for the split.

## What is here

- **Purdue network** — Ballard, Psomas, Seetharaman, Ellison, Simsek,
  Torres, Downey, Gray, Monaco, Fiechter, Khir
- **Prospect lists** — Indiana and Illinois produce operators
- **Scripts** — cold call (operator), discovery interview, connector call
- **Briefs** — tailored one-pagers, plus `brief_general_expert.pdf` as the
  reusable default

## The one question that gates everything

Q0 on the discovery script and question 0 on the cold-call script: do
operators ever reduce a customer commitment because supply is uncertain, or
do they promise and then scramble? If they always promise, the optimizer
has no decision to optimize regardless of how good the mathematics is.
