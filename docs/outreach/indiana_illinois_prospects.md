# Indiana & Illinois Prospects — Data-Access Targets

_Curated for the "get one real operator's historical data" push (Level 1
validation). Focus: small-to-large produce distributors, aggregators, and
supplier networks in IN/IL. Screened to avoid overlap/conflict with Guac
(retail-grocery demand forecasting) and to flag operators likely locked into
an existing ERP._

**Compiled:** 2026-07-11. Financials are third-party estimates (Growjo /
ZoomInfo / RocketReach / press) unless a press release is cited — treat as
directional, verify on the call.

---

## Guac-overlap screen (why these are safe)

Guac sells **AI demand forecasting + replenishment to grocery RETAILERS** (the
store shelf). Every prospect below is a **midstream distributor / aggregator /
food hub** — Guac's *customers' suppliers*, not Guac's customers. Different
buyer, different problem (two-sided supply risk + commitment, not shelf
sell-through). No known Guac contracts exist with any distributor (Guac's
public customers are grocery chains/delivery, incl. one ~300-store chain).
**Conflict risk: low across the board.**

Also flagged: operators owned by **FreshEdge** (Indianapolis Fruit, Piazza,
Garden Cut, etc.) already run a shared modern ERP → **poor data-access
targets** (decision authority + system lock-in). Listed at bottom as AVOID.
