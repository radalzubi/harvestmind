# Root-cause audit of the PR #114 FreshRetailNet pilot

Status: audit of head `b2654f4` (the 3,600-run pilot), 2026-10-19. The original evidence (runs, manifests,
RESULTS_fifty, FRN_PILOT.md) is preserved unchanged; every correction below is a new, separately labelled
configuration or a separately committed engine change. Classification: BUG (defect with a reproducible cause),
CALIBRATION (a constant tuned for another regime), EXPERIMENT_DESIGN (a benchmark choice that biased the reading),
EXPECTED_BEHAVIOR (the engine doing what it is specified to do), UNRESOLVED (not settled by this audit).

## Engine interventions in this pass

- **E1** `zaad/full/forecast.py`, `zaad/full/worlds.py`: seasonal candidate only on weekly periods. General (any non-weekly
  request), reproducible (finding 2), weekly behaviour unchanged, tested, canonical regression PASS.
- **E2** `zaad/full/replay.py`: clamp a kept order's expected receipt to its open units. General, reproducible (finding
  12), no effect when the quotient is exact, tested, canonical regression PASS.
- **E3** `zaad/full/policies.py`: joint, legal rule projections (finding 15). General (any request with lots, an MOV, a
  pallet cap or a cash budget), reproducible, no-op otherwise, tested, canonical regression PASS.
- **E3b** `zaad/full/policies.py`: the cash cap pays fixed order costs (finding 20). General, tested, no-op without them.
- **E4** `zaad/full/settlement.py`: sub-tolerance cash and debt residue snaps to zero (finding 16). General, tested,
  canonical regression PASS.
- **E5** `zaad/full/settlement.py`: `opening_assets` always in the ledger (finding 17). General, tested.
- Not changed, reported for the owner: the undeclared-storage big-M conditioning (finding 3) and the lateness slack
  calibration on sub-weekly periods (finding 7). No optimizer mathematics changed.

## What the corrections do and do not do

They remove two defects that corrupted inputs (2) or feasibility (3), stop a design choice from truncating runs (4),
and make the accounting and completion reporting fair (5, 6). They do not make ZAAD better: whether the production
planner adds value over the projected rules on this demand is decided by the re-run, reported in
`FRN_RERUN.md`, and the lateness calibration (7) remains the owner's question.
