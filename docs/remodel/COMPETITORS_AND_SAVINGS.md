## What cannot support a differentiation claim

Inventory reduction at maintained service, probabilistic demand, safety stock,
segmentation, scenario comparisons, ERP-connected recommendations, and human
planning workflows are established capabilities. The stock/service frontier
is not a novel category. No reviewed source establishes that competitors
cannot compare an incumbent plan, or that they universally lack safeguards.
Our explicit reproducible confirmation gate may be useful, but absence of a
public description of their gate is not proof of exclusivity.

MENA is not empty: Saudi GAINS/Naghi and Streamline/Panda, and UAE
Syncron/Al Masaood, are named deployed examples. They establish presence in
particular segments, not the proportion of distributors covered. No verified
country-by-vendor deployment census or modern-optimization penetration rate
was found. “Every large operator is served” and “small distributors have no
software” are both unsupported.

The numeric cases are vendor-selected successes, often bundled with data
cleanup, centralization, training and process redesign. We found no evidence
here allowing an unbiased population-average savings band by operator maturity.
**HarvestMind's demonstrated real-customer savings remain unknown**, not
10%, 20%, or the synthetic benchmark median. Model low/base/high business
cases as assumptions and require an operator-approved counterfactual.

## Capability classification

| HarvestMind component | Classification | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Forecasts, reorder points, service targets, ABC/XYZ, packs/MOQs, exports | A: table stakes | Necessary to compete; already documented in category |
| Standard LP/SP/CVaR/copulas | A: table stakes building blocks | Mathematics is available to competitors |
| Same-ledger replay with visible failed cells and abstention | B: useful differentiator | Auditable buying decision; not proof no competitor does it |
| Lightweight read-only diagnostic and explicit incremental ROI | B | Lower commitment hypothesis; actual deployment time must be measured |
| Supplier/route evidence tied to a feasible purchase alternative | B now, C only after data advantage | Public news is copyable; verified shipment outcomes and decisions may compound |
| Consented cross-customer outcome/calibration history | C: potential moat | Not currently present; consent and tenant isolation are prerequisites |
| Regional implementation/customer relationships | C potential | Operational asset, not a model property |
| New general ERP, generic risk news, RL before customer data | D: duplicative/premature | High build burden without demonstrated incremental demand |

Do not sell a relabelled statistical method as a moat. Sell a narrow,
auditable operating improvement and earn the data/network advantage later.
