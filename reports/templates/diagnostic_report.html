13. Assumptions & Limitations

This report is a diagnostic estimate, not a financial audit. All figures are derived from the CSV data uploaded to HarvestMind and should be verified against accounting records before use in external reporting.

AreaAssumption / Limitation
Waste loss valuation Dollar loss is taken from the estimated_value_lost field on each waste row. When that field is blank, HarvestMind falls back to quantity_lost × average unit_cost for that product (from supplier deliveries). Rows that cannot be valued (no estimated value AND no product-level unit cost) are counted in n_rows_unvalued and contribute $0 to the total — the total will understate true waste in that case.
Shortage loss valuation Missed quantity is requested − fulfilled − substituted, clamped at 0. Per row, loss = missed × sales_price; when the row's price is blank, HarvestMind falls back to the average sales price for that product across all orders. At-risk rows that cannot be priced are counted in n_at_risk_unvalued.
Lot expiry dates Near-expiry and expired flags depend entirely on the expiration_date column in the inventory lots file. HarvestMind does not infer expiry from arrival date + default shelf life. Missing expiry dates will cause lots to be treated as non-expiring.
Near-expiry threshold Lots expiring within 3 calendar days are flagged near-expiry. This threshold suits most fresh produce; operators with longer-shelf-life products (e.g., roots, dried goods) may wish to adjust.
Traceability scope Completeness is measured across 6 fields: lot ID, product ID, supplier ID, arrival date, quantity on hand, and unit. Full FSMA-204 compliance requires additional fields (harvest date, receiver info, transformation records) that are outside the current data model.
Savings annualization Period losses are scaled to a year by multiplying by 365 / period_days. ``period_days`` is auto-detected from the actual date range in the uploaded data (orders, deliveries, waste), clamped to a minimum of 7 days to prevent absurd extrapolation from tiny samples. This assumes the analyzed period is representative of the full year — seasonal operations should interpret this conservatively.
Savings reduction range Reported as a band: 15%–30% reduction of annualized addressable loss, sourced from USDA Agricultural Research Service (SR-77) benchmarks for produce hubs adopting improved inventory and fulfillment practices. The headline figure uses the high end (30%) for the conservative-side savings estimate. Actual savings depend on which recommendations are implemented.
Available-to-Promise (ATP) ATP = on_hand − expired − committed − near_expiry. Near-expiry stock (within 3 days of expiry) is excluded from ATP because committing near-expiry product to a delivery 3+ days out is a known cause of missed-order risk. Operators should source replenishment, not promise near-expiry product.
Supplier reliability — Unknown tier Suppliers with no ordered-quantity history in the data are returned with fill_rate = null and risk tier "Unknown". They are excluded from the risky-supplier count because the absence of data is not the same as good performance.
Data completeness Results are only as reliable as the uploaded data. A table with zero rows produces zero loss figures — not zero actual loss. All 5 staging tables should be populated for a meaningful diagnostic.
